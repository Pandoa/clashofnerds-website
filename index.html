PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: January 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy governs your use of the software application Clash of Nerds for mobile devices. Clash of Nerds is a knowledge quiz game designed for iOS and Android platforms.

1. Data Collection & Usage

Clash of Nerds does not collect personal information such as your name, email, or address directly. However, we use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob for advertisements. To provide personalized and non-personalized ads, AdMob may access and use:

  • Advertising Identifiers: IDFA (iOS) and Google Advertising ID (Android).
  • Device Info: Model, OS version, and mobile carrier.
  • Location: Coarse/IP-based location data for regional ad relevance.

You may opt out of personalized ads via your device system settings.

3. In-App Purchases

Clash of Nerds offers digital products for purchase. All transactions are handled by the respective app stores.

  • Apple App Store: Managed via your Apple ID and subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Store: Managed via your Google Account and subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

Clash of Nerds does not receive or store your credit card or billing information. We only receive confirmation of purchases necessary to unlock purchased features.

4. Your Rights

Regardless of your location, we respect your right to privacy. Since we do not store personal data on our own servers, app data can be cleared from your device and ad tracking can be managed through your device settings.

5. Contact

For questions regarding this policy, contact support@clashofnerds.com.
