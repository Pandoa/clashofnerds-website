The Ram — the first sign of the zodiac and home of the Golden Fleece.

Quick answer: Aries is a zodiac constellation known as the Ram, marked by a short crooked line of three bright stars: Hamal, Sheratan and Mesarthim.

What it looks like

Aries is a small zodiac constellation in the northern celestial hemisphere, drawn as a ram with a curved horn. Its three brightest stars form an easy-to-spot bent line.

ZodiacNorthern skyGolden Fleece

Brightest stars

Aries has one second-magnitude star, Hamal, plus two third-magnitude stars that complete the Ram's head.

Mythology and history

In Greek myth, Aries is the ram with the Golden Fleece that carried Phrixus to safety across the Hellespont.

The Golden Fleece

The ram was sent by Nephele to rescue her son Phrixus and daughter Helle from their stepmother Ino. Helle fell into the sea and drowned, giving her name to the Hellespont. Phrixus reached Colchis, sacrificed the ram to Zeus, and hung its fleece in a sacred grove — the prize Jason and the Argonauts later sought.

How to find it

Look for a short bent line of stars between the Pleiades and the Great Square of Pegasus, easiest on autumn and winter evenings.

  • Find Hamal first — it's the brightest star in the region.
  • Follow the line east to Sheratan and Mesarthim to trace the Ram's head.
  • Use the Great Square of Pegasus as a pointer to the west.

Aries contains few bright deep-sky objects. NGC 772 is a large spiral galaxy, and NGC 1156 is an irregular dwarf galaxy.
