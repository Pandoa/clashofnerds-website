Top 10 Best Quiz Apps 2026

Updated November 2026 · 8 min read

Looking for the best quiz app to test your knowledge against friends or strangers? We ranked the top trivia and quiz apps based on question quality, multiplayer features, language support, and overall fun. Whether you want fast-paced 1v1 duels or group trivia nights, here are the best options—with Clash of Nerds taking the crown for pure head-to-head brain battles.

1. Clash of Nerds: Duel Quiz — 5/5 ⭐

iOS · Android · Free
Visit clashofnerds.com →

Clash of Nerds is the definitive 1v1 trivia experience. It strips away the bloat of modern quiz apps and focuses entirely on what matters: fast, competitive brain duels across geography, flags, astronomy, chemistry, and general knowledge.

Pros

  • True 1v1 duels — every match is a head-to-head battle, not a solo grind
  • 20 languages supported — play against opponents worldwide in your native language
  • Deep topic variety — countries, capitals, flags, planets, periodic table, constellations, and more
  • Clean, distraction-free interface — no energy systems, no forced ads between questions
  • Completely free — no subscription required to access core gameplay

Cons

  • Newer app with a smaller player pool than legacy giants like Trivia Crack
  • No team or group mode (focus is strictly on duels)
Verdict: If you want pure 1v1 trivia competition without paywalls or ad interruptions, Clash of Nerds is the best quiz app available today.

2. Trivia Crack — 3.5/5 ⭐

iOS · Android · Free (ads) / Subscription
View on App Store →

Trivia Crack is the household name in mobile trivia, with over 100 million downloads. It offers a wheel-based question format covering six categories: history, geography, entertainment, art, science, and sports. It supports friend challenges, worldwide rankings, and AI-assisted quiz creation . However, recent user reviews consistently report aggressive ad frequency that has made the game difficult to enjoy .

Pros

  • Massive player base — easy to find opponents
  • Six classic categories plus user-generated and AI-assisted quizzes
  • Friend challenges, rankings, and multiple game modes
  • Available on iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, and iMessage

Cons

  • Heavy ad load — users report ads after every missed question, sometimes mid-question
  • User-generated and AI questions are not consistently fact-checked
  • 13+ age rating due to social features
  • Ad-free experience requires a paid subscription
Verdict: A legacy choice with a huge community, but the ad experience has degraded significantly. Consider only if you don't mind paying to remove ads.

3. Kahoot! — 3/5 ⭐

iOS · Android · Web · Free (limited) / Paid plans
Visit kahoot.com →

Kahoot! is less a competitive trivia app and more a classroom and presentation tool. You can create your own quizzes and host live games for groups, making it excellent for teachers and trivia hosts . The app supports playing live kahoots, creating your own, and studying with flashcards . However, it is not designed for 1v1 competitive duels, and the free version limits participants and question types.

Pros

  • Excellent for group and classroom play — host on a big screen
  • Create custom quizzes easily with templates and images
  • Leaderboards and immediate feedback drive engagement
  • Works across devices, including SharePlay on FaceTime

Cons

  • Not designed for 1v1 competitive duels
  • Free version limits advanced question types and features
  • Requires stable internet for live play
  • Learning curve for hosts and teachers
Verdict: The best choice if you're a teacher or trivia host. Not a replacement for competitive 1v1 trivia apps.

4. SongPop — 2.5/5 ⭐

iOS · Android · Free (with IAP)
View on App Store →

SongPop is a music recognition game rather than a general knowledge quiz. It offers real-time PvP matches, tournaments, and genre playlists with over 100,000 licensed song clips . The appeal is narrow: if you love music trivia, it's fun; if you want general knowledge competition, look elsewhere. User complaints about bugs and monetization are also a concern .

Pros

  • Massive licensed music library — 100K+ clips
  • Real-time PvP, async duels, and tournaments
  • Genre variety — pop, rock, hip-hop, K-pop, Latin, and more

Cons

  • Music only — no general knowledge categories
  • User reports of bugs, monetization issues, and lost progress
  • Not usable in silent environments
  • 13+ rating
Verdict: Great for music lovers, irrelevant for general trivia fans.

5. Who Wants to Be a Millionaire? — 2.5/5 ⭐

iOS · Android · Free (with IAP)
View on App Store →

Who Wants to Be a Millionaire? follows the TV show format with lifelines, city progression, and daily leaderboards. Its standout feature is explicit offline support, making it the best choice for solo play while traveling . The app advertises over 10,000 trivia questions and works without internet .

Pros

  • Offline mode — play without internet
  • Familiar TV show format with lifelines
  • 4+ age rating — family friendly
  • 10,000+ questions across categories

Cons

  • Solo only — no real multiplayer competition
  • Basic format with limited replay value
  • In-app purchases for hints and ad-free mode
Verdict: The best offline solo quiz, but not a competitive multiplayer experience.

6. QuizUp — 2/5 ⭐

iOS · Free (with heavy subscription prompts)
View on App Store →

QuizUp was once a beloved trivia app with real-time multiplayer and a vast category library. The current version is developed by Two4Tea and offers quick matches, power-ups, and leaderboards . However, the classic QuizUp was discontinued in 2021, and the current listing is a different product using the name. Users report aggressive subscription prompts and in-app purchases ranging from $0.99 to $29.99 .

Pros

  • Real-time multiplayer matches against real players
  • Power-ups (Jokers, Wands) add strategic depth
  • Leaderboards and friend challenges

Cons

  • Not the original QuizUp — the classic app was discontinued in 2021
  • Aggressive in-app purchases and subscription prompts
  • User-generated content means variable question accuracy
  • Reports of server reliability and notification overload
Verdict: A shadow of its former self. Skip it unless you're curious about the new version.

7. The Chase – World Tour — 2/5 ⭐

iOS · Android · Free (with IAP)
View on App Store →

The Chase recreates the UK TV quiz show format with recognizable Chasers, team mechanics, and weekly leaderboards . It's a decent adaptation, but some users report U.K.-centric questions that may confuse international players . The app features Chasers from the UK, USA, and Australia .

Pros

  • Authentic TV show adaptation with real Chasers
  • Team mechanics and weekly leaderboards
  • Regular content updates

Cons

  • Questions may be U.K.-centric
  • 13+ rating with simulated gambling themes
  • In-app purchases for gold and energy
  • Not a pure 1v1 duel format
Verdict: Fun for fans of the TV show, but the regional bias limits its global appeal.

8. DK Quiz — 2/5 ⭐

iOS · Free

DK Quiz is a trivia app that covers a variety of topics from food to music to inventions. You can play solo or challenge friends in turn-based head-to-head matches . It's a solid option for casual play, but lacks the depth and competitive features of dedicated 1v1 trivia apps.

Pros

  • Wide variety of trivia categories
  • Solo mode with leaderboards
  • Turn-based friend challenges

Cons

  • Limited competitive multiplayer features
  • Basic presentation
  • Less active community than major competitors
Verdict: A decent casual trivia app, but not a serious contender for competitive play.

9. TriviaPlaza — 1.5/5 ⭐

Web only · Free (ad-supported)
Visit triviaplaza.com →

TriviaPlaza is a browser-based quiz site with a wide variety of topics including pop music, movies, geography, science, and history . It is completely free and offers instant feedback, but it lacks any social features, multiplayer, or a mobile app. The site design feels dated compared to modern quiz apps.

Pros

  • Free access to a wide range of quiz categories
  • Simple, easy-to-navigate interface
  • No download required — works in browser

Cons

  • No multiplayer or social features
  • Dated design and user experience
  • No mobile app — web only
  • Limited replay value
Verdict: A basic web quiz site. Fine for a quick solo session, but not a serious competitor.

10. Socrative — 1/5 ⭐

Web · iOS · Android · Free (limited) / Paid
Visit socrative.com →

Socrative is a classroom assessment tool, not a consumer trivia app. It provides real-time feedback and automatic scoring for teachers , but it has no competitive multiplayer and is not intended for casual trivia play. Students join via a teacher's room code and answer formative assessment questions .

Pros

  • Real-time feedback and automatic grading for teachers
  • Works across devices
  • Good for formative assessment and exit tickets

Cons

  • Not a trivia game — strictly a classroom tool
  • No competitive multiplayer
  • Requires a teacher to set up and host
  • Irrelevant for casual quiz players
Verdict: Only relevant for teachers. Consumers looking for a quiz app should skip it entirely.
