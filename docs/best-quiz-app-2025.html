Top 10 Best Quiz Apps 2025

Updated November 2025 · 8 min read

Looking for the best quiz app to test your knowledge against friends or strangers? We ranked the top trivia and quiz apps for 2025 based on question quality, multiplayer features, language support, and overall fun. Whether you want fast-paced 1v1 duels or group trivia nights, here are the best options—with Clash of Nerds taking the crown for pure head-to-head brain battles.

1. Clash of Nerds: Duel Quiz — 5/5 ⭐

iOS · Android · Free
Visit clashofnerds.com →

Clash of Nerds is the definitive 1v1 trivia experience. It strips away the bloat of modern quiz apps and focuses entirely on what matters: fast, competitive brain duels across geography, flags, astronomy, chemistry, and general knowledge. With 20 languages and a clean interface, it's the purest head-to-head trivia app of 2025.

Pros

  • True 1v1 duels — every match is head-to-head
  • 20 languages — play against opponents worldwide
  • Deep topic variety — countries, capitals, flags, planets, periodic table, constellations
  • No energy systems or forced ads
  • Completely free

Cons

  • Newer app with a smaller player pool
  • No team or group mode
Verdict: If you want pure 1v1 trivia competition without paywalls or ad interruptions, Clash of Nerds is the best quiz app available today.

2. GeoGuessr — 4/5 ⭐

iOS · Android · Web · Free (limited) / Paid
Visit geoguessr.com →

GeoGuessr is the undisputed king of geography quiz games. You're dropped into a random Street View location and must pinpoint where you are on the map. The Duels and Battle Royale modes deliver genuine competitive tension, and the skill ceiling is enormous. The free tier is limited to a few minutes per day, and the subscription is pricey, but for geography fans it's unmatched.

Pros

  • Duels and Battle Royale modes are genuinely competitive
  • Massive skill ceiling — real geography mastery matters
  • Huge community and endless user-made maps

Cons

  • Free tier limited to a few minutes per day
  • Subscription required for serious play
  • Geography-only — no general knowledge
Verdict: The best geography quiz game ever made. If you love maps, it's essential. If you want broad trivia, look elsewhere.

3. Finto — 3.5/5 ⭐

iOS · Android · Free
Visit finto.app →

Finto is a party trivia game with a twist: you don't need to know the answer. You bluff one. Players write fake answers to real questions, then everyone votes on which is true. It's social, hilarious, and works with up to 7 players in the same room. Not a competitive 1v1 app, but one of the best group trivia experiences on mobile.

Pros

  • Brilliant bluffing mechanic — knowledge optional, creativity required
  • Up to 7 players in the same room
  • Hilarious social experience

Cons

  • Group-only — no solo or 1v1 online play
  • Not a competitive trivia app
  • Requires everyone in the same room
Verdict: The best party trivia game on mobile. Perfect for game nights, irrelevant for solo competitive play.

4. Sporcle Party — 3/5 ⭐

iOS · Android · Free
Visit sporcle.com/party →

Sporcle Party brings the legendary Sporcle quiz library to a group format. You host a game, everyone joins on their phone, and you race through categories together. The question quality is excellent—Sporcle has been curating trivia for over 15 years. But it's a host-led group experience, not a competitive 1v1 app.

Pros

  • Backed by Sporcle's massive, high-quality question library
  • Easy group setup — everyone joins on their phone
  • Wide category variety

Cons

  • Host-led group format only
  • No 1v1 competitive mode
  • Requires a host to run the game
Verdict: Excellent for group trivia nights. Not a competitive 1v1 app.

5. Yabaleli — 3/5 ⭐

iOS · Android · Free
Visit yabaleli.com →

Yabaleli is an AI-powered trivia app with real-time 1v1 matches across 11 languages. The AI generates questions on the fly, which means fresh content every session—but also inconsistent quality. It's a promising concept with a small but growing player base. Worth trying if you want something different from the polished legacy apps.

Pros

  • AI-generated questions — fresh content every session
  • Real-time 1v1 matches
  • 11 languages supported

Cons

  • AI questions can be inconsistent in quality
  • Small player base
  • Less polished than established competitors
Verdict: A promising AI trivia concept. Worth a try, but not yet a top-tier competitive app.

6. TimeGuessr — 2.5/5 ⭐

Web · Free
Visit timeguessr.com →

TimeGuessr is GeoGuessr's historical cousin. You're shown a historical photograph and must guess both the year and the location. It's a daily puzzle format, free to play, and genuinely educational. But it's web-only, solo-focused, and lacks the competitive multiplayer depth of GeoGuessr.

Pros

  • Unique historical + geography combination
  • Free daily puzzle format
  • Genuinely educational

Cons

  • Web-only — no mobile app
  • Solo-focused — limited multiplayer
  • One puzzle per day limits replay value
Verdict: A clever daily puzzle for history buffs. Not a competitive multiplayer app.

7. Erudite — 2.5/5 ⭐

iOS · Android · Free
View on Google Play →

Erudite is a solo general knowledge quiz app with over 3,000 levels and a clean, ad-light interface. It's a solid option for commuters who want to grind through questions without multiplayer pressure. But it's solo-only, with no competitive 1v1 mode and limited social features.

Pros

  • 3,000+ levels of general knowledge questions
  • Clean, ad-light interface
  • Good for solo commuter play

Cons

  • Solo-only — no competitive 1v1
  • Limited social features
  • Repetitive after extended play
Verdict: A decent solo trivia grinder. Not a competitive multiplayer app.

8. Quiz8 — 2/5 ⭐

Web · Free
Visit quiz8.ai →

Quiz8 is a browser-based multiplayer trivia game with AI-generated questions. You can create a room, invite friends, and play through themed rounds. The AI generation means fresh content, but also variable quality and occasional factual errors. It's a fun party trick, not a serious competitive app.

Pros

  • AI-generated questions — fresh every session
  • Browser-based — no download required
  • Easy room creation for groups

Cons

  • AI question quality is inconsistent
  • Browser-only — no mobile app
  • Small player base
Verdict: A fun browser-based party game. Not a serious competitive trivia app.

9. Trivia 360 — 2/5 ⭐

iOS · Free
View on App Store →

Trivia 360 offers multiple modes, global leaderboards, and a wide category selection. It's a functional solo trivia app, but it lacks the polish and competitive depth of the top entries. The interface feels dated and the question pool, while broad, isn't as deep as Sporcle or Clash of Nerds.

Pros

  • Multiple game modes
  • Global leaderboards
  • Wide category selection

Cons

  • Dated interface
  • No real competitive 1v1 mode
  • Question quality varies
Verdict: A functional but forgettable solo trivia app.

10. Trivia Duel — 1.5/5 ⭐

Web · Free
Visit triviaduel.com →

Trivia Duel is a web-based trivia game that lets you play solo or against friends. The concept is straightforward—answer questions faster than your opponent—but the execution feels unfinished. The question pool is shallow, the UI is basic, and there's no mobile app. It's a proof of concept, not a finished product.

Pros

  • Simple, fast-paced format
  • Play against friends
  • No download required

Cons

  • Shallow question pool
  • Basic UI
  • No mobile app
Verdict: An unfinished web prototype. Skip it.
