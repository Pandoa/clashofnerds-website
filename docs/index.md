# Clash of Nerds — 1v1 Quiz Duel

> Clash of Nerds is a free 1v1 trivia quiz game for iOS and Android. Players duel real opponents on geography, science, flags, capitals, astronomy, and chemistry — answering fast, earning trophies, and climbing the ranks. Available in 20 languages.

## Quick facts

- **What it is:** a real-time 1v1 knowledge quiz duel (PvP trivia), not a solo quiz app.
- **Platforms:** iOS and Android.
- **Price:** free to play, with optional in-app purchases.
- **Topics:** countries, capitals, flags, astronomy, chemistry, general knowledge.
- **Languages:** 20.
- **Progression:** earn trophies during duels; win duels to unlock new topics.
- **Website role:** landing page plus free browsable reference pages for world flags and constellations.

## Gameplay topics

- **Countries:** identify and answer questions about world countries.
- **Capitals:** match countries to their capital cities.
- **Flags:** [Flag reference index](https://clashofnerds.com/flags/) — every country flag with colors, meanings, and adoption dates.
- **Astronomy:** planets, stars, and space.
- **Chemistry:** the periodic table, elements, and reactions.
- **Constellations:** [Constellation reference index](https://clashofnerds.com/constellations/) — all 88 constellations with charts and mythology.

## Privacy and data

- **Last updated:** August 31, 2026.
- **Accounts:** no user accounts or profiles are hosted on Clash of Nerds servers; no name, email, or address is collected directly.
- **Analytics:** Google Firebase Analytics — product interaction (app launches, screen views, taps, gameplay progression) and device identifiers (IDFV).
- **Advertising:** Google AdMob — advertising identifiers (IDFA on iOS, Google Advertising ID on Android), coarse/IP-based location, and ad interaction data. Data may be used for cross-app tracking; users can restrict this in device settings (Apple App Tracking Transparency).
- **Purchases:** all transactions are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play; the app never receives card numbers or billing details.
- **Data deletion:** clearing app data or uninstalling the app removes local history.
